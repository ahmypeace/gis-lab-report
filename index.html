About This Portfolio

Peace Amaka Onebunne is a PhD student in communication at Temple University. The work collected here moves from mapping census variables at two geographic scales, through vector overlay for siting a healthy food store, to an interactive web map built with the ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript.

Featured Analysis: Siting a Healthy Food Store

Qualifying areas sit inside a Philadelphia Empowerment Zone, within 2,000 feet of a SEPTA subway or Regional Rail station, and outside 1,200-foot buffers around Farmers Markets and Healthy Corner Stores. Eight candidate polygons remained after the overlay, and Candidate 4, at about 219.68 acres, is the largest contiguous area and the recommended site.
